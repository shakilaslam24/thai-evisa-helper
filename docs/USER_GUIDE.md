# DreamFly CRM — User Guide

A practical walkthrough of running the business day to day. Written for the whole
team, not just administrators.

---

## 1. Signing in

Open the system address in any browser (phone, tablet or computer) and sign in
with the email and password your administrator gave you.

- **Change your password** from the menu behind your name, top right.
- **Theme** can be switched between System, Light and Dark from the same menu.
- Your session lasts seven days on that device.

---

## 2. The dashboard

The dashboard is your morning briefing. It shows:

- total leads, and how many arrived today
- **today's follow-up** and **overdue follow-up** — start here every morning
- today's meetings
- customers, active files, and files by stage (processing, interview pending,
  documents pending, approved, rejected, completed)
- B2B partners, invoices, pending payments and the monthly sales summary

Two views are available:

- **Whole company** — everything across the team
- **My work** — only leads, follow-ups and meetings assigned to you

Every number is a link. Clicking "Overdue follow-up" opens the follow-up queue
already filtered to overdue items.

---

## 3. Leads — the daily engine

### Adding a lead

**Leads → + Add lead.** Record the name, phone, WhatsApp, email, address, where the
enquiry came from, what service and country they are interested in, and how hot the
lead is.

Set **Next follow-up date & time** while you are there. This is the single most
important field: it creates a real follow-up task and the system will remind you.

### Working a lead

Open any lead to get its full profile. From there you can:

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Call** | Dials the number on a phone |
| **WhatsApp** | Opens WhatsApp with that number |
| **Add follow-up** | Schedules the next contact and reminds the assigned staff member |
| **Schedule meeting** | Books a meeting and moves the lead to "Meeting Fixed" |
| **Status dropdown** | Moves the lead through the pipeline |
| **Convert to customer** | Creates the full customer profile |

Everything you do is written into the **activity timeline** at the bottom — who did
what, and when. Use the note box there to record call notes.

### The lead pipeline

`New Lead → Contacted → Follow-up Running → Interested → Meeting Fixed → Converted`

Dead ends are `Not Interested` and `Closed`.

### Converting

When the client commits, press **Convert to customer**. Contact details carry over
automatically; add the passport number and date of birth now or later. The lead is
marked Converted and permanently linked to the new customer profile.

---

## 4. Follow-up — never lose a client

**Follow-up** is one queue for every scheduled contact across leads, customers,
files and partners.

Use the chips at the top to switch between **Due today**, **Overdue**, **Upcoming**
and **All**, or press your own name to see only your items.

When you have made the call, press **Complete**, write what happened, and — this is
the important part — set the **next follow-up date** in the same box. The system
closes the old task and opens the next one in a single step, so a client can never
fall through the gap.

> **Example:** you set a reminder for a client on 25 June at 4 PM. At 4 PM the bell
> in the top bar shows a notification, the item turns up in "Due today", and if you
> miss it, it moves to "Overdue" and stays there until you deal with it.

---

## 5. Meetings

Schedule from a lead, a customer or a partner profile, or directly from
**Meetings → + Schedule meeting**.

Set the type (Office Visit, Phone Call, Video Call, Follow-up Meeting), who is
handling it, and **how many minutes before** you want to be reminded. When that
window opens, a notification appears.

Mark meetings **Done** afterwards, or set them to Rescheduled or Cancelled.

---

## 6. Customers

A customer is a confirmed client. The profile holds their given name, surname, date
of birth, passport number, NID, gender, nationality, phone, WhatsApp, email,
address, service type, country and any special notes.

From a customer profile you can open a **new file**, raise an **invoice**, upload
**documents**, schedule follow-ups and meetings, and read the full history.

Search by name, passport number, phone or email — the top bar searches every module
at once.

---

## 7. Files / Cases

Every application is a file with its own reference number (`DF-2026-0001`), created
automatically.

### Tracking a file

Files carry the customer, passport, country, service and file type, the assigned
staff member and linked B2B partner, plus the key dates: submission, embassy/VFS,
interview and completion.

Change the status from the dropdown at the top of the file:

`Draft · Documents Pending · Ready for Submission · Submitted · Under Processing ·
Additional Documents Required · Interview Called · Approved · Rejected · Delivered ·
Completed · Hold`

Approved, Rejected, Delivered and Completed automatically stamp a completion date.

### The document checklist

Every new file starts with a standard checklist — passport copy, photo, NID, bank
statement, air ticket, hotel booking. Mark each one **Missing**, **Received** or
**Not Required**, and add your own items for unusual cases.

Uploading a document in a matching category ticks the checklist line for you.

Files that are ready or already submitted but still have missing documents raise a
notification, so nothing goes to the embassy incomplete.

---

## 8. B2B Partners

**B2B Partners** holds your agents and sub-agents: partner name, company, both
addresses, personal and company phone numbers, WhatsApp, email, trade licence,
NID/passport, commission rate and any special agreement.

### The partner dashboard

Opening a partner shows, at a glance:

- total files submitted, how many are under processing, approved, rejected
- interview cases and documents still missing
- total billed, total paid and outstanding balance
- the full list of their files, invoice history and payment history
- notes and communication history

### Adding a file under a partner

Press **+ Add file under partner**. Enter the traveller's passport number, surname,
given name, date of birth, phone, email, address, country, service type, status,
notes and a reference number (leave it blank to generate one).

The system creates the customer profile and opens the file in one step. If that
passport number already exists, the new file is added to the existing customer
instead of creating a duplicate.

---

## 9. Documents

Upload from any customer, file, partner or lead profile: passport copies, NID,
photos, bank statements, trade licences, NOCs, invitation letters, tickets, hotel
bookings, or anything else.

Images, PDF, Word and Excel files up to 15 MB are accepted. Every document can be
previewed in the browser or downloaded.

**Documents** in the menu lists everything uploaded across the whole system, so you
can find a file even when you cannot remember which record it sits under.

---

## 10. Invoices

Create an invoice from a customer profile, a file, a partner profile, or from
**Invoices → + Create invoice**.

- The invoice number is generated automatically (`DF-INV-2026-0001`); the prefix is
  set in Settings.
- Add as many line items as you need, each with a quantity and unit price.
- Apply a discount and tax; the totals update as you type.
- **Print** produces a clean invoice on paper. **Download PDF** opens the same print
  dialog — choose "Save as PDF" as the destination.

Your company name, address, phone numbers, email, payment terms and footer note come
from **Settings → Company details** and **Invoice template**.

At the bottom of every invoice, the name of the person who raised it is printed in
place of a signature, and beside it a **QR code**. A client scans that code with
their phone camera, lands on the tracking page and checks their own file with their
passport number and name — so they can stop calling the office for an update. The
link behind the code, and whether it is printed at all, is set in
**Settings → Tracking link & QR**.

Payment status is `Unpaid`, `Partial Paid` or `Paid`, and updates itself as payments
are recorded.

---

## 11. Payments and refunds

Press **Record payment** on any unpaid invoice. Enter the amount, method (Cash,
bKash, Nagad, Bank Transfer, Cheque, Card or Other), date and transaction reference.

### Giving money back

Open the invoice, find the payment in the **Payments and refunds** list and press
**Refund**. Enter how much is going back and why — the reason is required,
because it is the only thing that later tells a cancelled trip apart from a
mistake.

The refund appears as its own line next to the original receipt. Nothing is
erased: the books show BDT 5,000 received on Tuesday and BDT 5,000 returned on
Friday, which is what actually happened. The invoice goes back to Partial Paid
or Unpaid on its own.

Anyone on the team can issue a refund. **Deleting** a payment record is different
— that is for an administrator fixing a mis-typed entry, and it is not how you
return money to a client.

Part payments are supported — the invoice moves to "Partial Paid" and shows the
outstanding balance. The system will not let you record more than is outstanding.

The file's payment status updates automatically from its invoices.

**Payments** in the menu lists every amount received, filterable by method, partner
and date range.

---

## 12. Reports

Twelve reports, each with a date range and a **CSV export** for your accountant:

- daily lead report
- monthly lead conversion
- lead source performance
- follow-up pending
- active file report
- country-wise report
- approved vs rejected
- partner-wise file report
- invoice report
- payment due report
- payment collection report
- staff performance

Quick range buttons cover this month, last 30 days, last 90 days and this year.

---

## 13. Staff performance

Admins and managers can see, per team member and per period: leads handled,
customers converted, conversion rate, follow-ups completed, overdue items, meetings,
files created, approvals, rejections and revenue invoiced.

---

## 14. Notifications

The bell in the top bar shows anything needing attention. The system checks every
minute and raises alerts for:

- follow-ups due now, and follow-ups that have gone overdue
- meetings entering their reminder window
- interview dates within the next three days
- invoices past their payment due date
- files that are ready or submitted but still missing documents

Each alert links straight to the record. Any type can be switched off under
**Settings → Notification settings**.

## 15. Settings (administrators)

| Tab | What you control |
| --- | --- |
| **Company details** | Name, tagline, address, three phone numbers, email, website, logo |
| **Invoice template** | Number prefix, currency, payment terms, footer note |
| **Tracking link & QR** | The public tracking address, and the QR code printed on invoices |
| **Countries, services & lists** | Country list, service categories, lead sources, document categories |
| **Users & roles** | Add users, set roles, deactivate accounts, reset passwords |
| **Notification settings** | Switch each reminder type on or off |

Removing an item from a list hides it from future dropdowns but never changes
records that already use it.

### The tracking link

Leave **Public tracking link** empty and the QR code points at whatever address the
system is being used on, which is right while you are testing. Once your own domain
is live, put the full address in — `https://crm.yourcompany.com/track.html` — and
save. Every invoice printed from then on carries the new link; invoices already
handed out still point at the old one, so keep the old address working for a while.

The preview under the field shows the code as it will print, and **Open the tracking
page** checks the link before you commit to it.

### Roles

Everyone on the team can do the same day-to-day work — take a lead, add a customer,
open a file, raise an invoice, collect a payment. What changes with the role is how
much of the company a person can see, and who is allowed to delete something.

| Role | Sees | May delete |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Admin** | The whole company, plus users and settings | Files, documents, customers |
| **Manager** | The whole company — pipeline and money | No |
| **Accounts / Finance** | The whole company's money; own work in the pipeline | No |
| **Staff / Executive** | Only their own work and their own sales | No |
| **B2B Partner** | Only the files and invoices under their own partner account (read-only) | No |

Staff performance and the Partner-wise report are shown to admins and managers only.

The last active administrator cannot be demoted or deactivated, so you can never
lock yourself out.

---

## 16. A suggested daily routine

1. **Open the dashboard.** Clear "Overdue follow-up" first, then "Today's follow-up".
2. **Check today's meetings** and prepare for them.
3. **Work new leads** — call, note the outcome, set the next follow-up date.
4. **Update file statuses** as embassies and partners come back to you.
5. **Record any payments** received during the day.
6. **End of month:** run the invoice, payment due and staff performance reports.

---

## 17. Tips

- The top-bar search covers leads, customers, files, partners and invoices at once —
  a passport number is usually the fastest way in.
- Filtered lists are bookmarkable. Filter to "my overdue follow-ups" once and save
  the link.
- Always complete a follow-up with the next one scheduled; that is what keeps the
  pipeline alive.
- Use the note box on every timeline to record what was said on a call. Six months
  later it is the only record you will have.
